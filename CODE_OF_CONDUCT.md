# Code of conduct

Oversay follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/) as its code of conduct. In short:

- Be respectful and welcoming to everyone, whatever their background or experience.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Focus on what is best for the project and the people who use it.
- No harassment, insults, discrimination, or publishing other people's private information.

This applies in issues, pull requests, discussions and any other space that is part of the project.

## Reporting and enforcement

To report behaviour that breaks these rules, email **support@oversay.app**. Reports are handled privately by the maintainer. The maintainer may edit or remove comments, close or lock threads, and ban people from the project, and will explain a decision when asked.
