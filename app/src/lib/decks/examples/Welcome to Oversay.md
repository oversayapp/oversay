# Welcome to Oversay

## Welcome [15s, intro]
- Oversay puts your notes on screen, one card at a time
- This card has 15 seconds: its time turns amber for the last 10
- Then the next card comes up by itself

## Your notes, only for you [2m]
- A card shows its points over your call, your slides or your demo
- Hidden from screen sharing and recordings: your audience never sees it
- Each card can have a time, or none
- The timeline shows the whole deck and where you are in it

## Decks, made your way [2m]
- Write a deck in Oversay, or as a Markdown file: each `## ` heading is a card
- Times go after the heading: `[3m]`, `[30s]`, or `[~5m]` as a rough guide
- Kinds colour the cards: intro, topic, demo, outro
- Tags and audience keep many decks easy to find

## The Library [2m]
- Decks and workspaces live in one place: the Library on the left rail
- Recent, Pinned and This week list both; Unsorted shows the decks in no workspace
- A workspace groups the decks for one project, course or talk; a deck can be in several
- This deck is in the workspace "Oversay talk", beside the conference talk

## Make anything with + New [1m, demo]
- + New, or ⌘N, offers a blank deck, a deck from a template, or a workspace
- ⌘K is the command bar: it finds decks, workspaces, templates and settings, and does things
- Looking at a workspace? A new deck goes straight into it

## Start from a template [1m]
- 24 starters for talks, meetings, lessons and more, on the Templates page
- Pick one and its cards, kinds and times become your deck, ready to fill in
- Save your own once you have a shape you like

## Stay in control [1m, demo]
- Next and Previous from anywhere: ⌃⌥→ and ⌃⌥←
- Pause and resume: ⌃⌥Space; end: ⌃⌥E or the card's ✕
- Turn auto-advance off and a card waits for Next, counting on in red

## See how it went [1m]
- When a deck ends, its session shows how long each card really took against its plan
- Sessions keeps every run; turn on Record my voice to get a transcript
- Your AI can then review the run with you

## Let your AI write your decks [1m]
- Install the Oversay skill from the AI page, then tell your AI what the meeting is about
- It writes the deck straight into the Library, ready to show

## Make your own [30s, outro]
- Press ⌘N and start a deck
- These examples can be deleted any time; Settings puts them back
