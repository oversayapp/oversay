# Why bees matter

## Hook [30s, intro]
- Picture a supermarket with no pollinated food
- About one in three mouthfuls depends on pollinators (check my source)
> Pause after the first sentence.

## What bees do [1m30s]
- Pollination: moving pollen between flowers so plants make seed and fruit
- Honeybees, bumblebees and hundreds of wild solitary species
- Wild bees do much of the work, not only honeybees

## Why they are declining [2m]
- Habitat loss: fewer wildflowers and hedgerows
- Pesticides, especially neonicotinoids
- Disease and parasites such as the varroa mite
- A changing climate shifts when flowers and bees appear

## What helps [2m]
- More wildflowers, and mowing less
- Fewer pesticides, applied more carefully
- Nesting sites: bare soil, hollow stems, bee hotels
- Gardens, school grounds and balconies all count

## The chart [1m, demo]
- Show the slide with the decline in wild bee species
- Say where the numbers come from before the audience asks

## Close [30s, outro]
- Bees feed us, and we can help them
- One thing to do this week: leave a patch of lawn unmown

## Questions [open]
- Honeybees versus wild bees?
- Do bee hotels really work?
