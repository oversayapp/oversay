# Notes on screen, not in your way

## Intro [2m, intro]
- Who I am, and where I give talks
- The promise: you will never lose your place on a call again
> Slow down. Smile. Don't start with the product.

## The problem [4m]
- Notes in another window mean looking away from your audience
- Sharing the wrong window shows your notes to everyone
- Slides full of text are notes for you, not for them
- Memorising a talk works until the one question you didn't plan for

## The approach [4m]
- One card at a time, over whatever is on your screen
- Hidden from screen sharing and recordings: only you see it
- Each card has a time and a kind; the timeline shows where you are
- Plain Markdown files on your computer: no account, no cloud

## Demo: a deck in two minutes [6m, demo]
- Press ⌘N, choose a deck from a template, and name it
- Show it: the card appears over the slides
- Next and Previous from the keyboard, with the clock turning amber
- End it and look at the session: planned against actual, per card
> If the screen share stalls, say so and carry on with the slides.

## What it is not [2m]
- Not a teleprompter: you decide what to say, it holds the points
- Not slides: nobody but you sees a card
- Not only for talks: calls, stand-ups, interviews, lessons

## Wrap-up [2m, outro]
- Notes on screen, hidden from the room, with the time beside them
- Try it on your next call; the example decks show the rest
- Thank you

## Questions [open, outro]
- Does it work with my video app? Yes, over any window
- Where are my decks kept? In Documents, as Markdown
