# Interview talking points

## Opening [intro]
- Who I am in one sentence
- The two things I am proudest of
- Why this role, now

## A time I led something
- The situation, in one line
- What I decided and why
- The result, with a number if I have one

## A time something went wrong
- What happened, without blaming anyone
- What I did in the first hour
- What I changed afterwards

## A time I disagreed
- The other view, stated fairly
- How I made my case
- What we agreed, and what I learned

## Why this company
- Something specific about their product I have used
- How my work fits their next year
- Who I would learn from

## My questions for them
- What does success look like in six months?
- How does the team make decisions when it disagrees?
- What is the hardest thing about the role?

## Salary and start date
- My range, said once and calmly
- Start date: earliest and latest
- Notice period

## Close [outro]
- Thank them for their time
- Ask about next steps and when I will hear
