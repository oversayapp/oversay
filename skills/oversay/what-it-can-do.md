# What the Oversay skill can do

Oversay puts your notes on screen beside a call or a talk, one card at a time, where only you can see them. This skill lets me make those cards for you, so you can ask me in plain words and the deck is waiting in Oversay.

## Make a note for a meeting

Tell me who it's with and what you want to cover. I'll make a short deck with a card for each subject and a few points on each. You move through it with Next.

> Create an Oversay note for my catch-up with Sam tomorrow: the hiring plan and the offsite.

## Timed talking points

Give me times and I'll put them on the cards. Oversay counts each card down beside your call and moves to the next one by itself.

> Create talking points for my stand-up today: one topic per project, timed 3, 4 and 5 minutes, plus a 1-minute intro.

## Add to a deck you already have

Give me a few points, or paste in some Markdown, and I'll add it to the deck. Everything already in it stays as it was.

> Here's some Markdown, add it to my Weekly standup deck in Oversay.

## Find your decks

> Which Oversay decks do I have?

## Group decks into workspaces

Oversay keeps the decks for one project, one day of a course or one talk together in a workspace, and tags workspaces so you can see all your research or all your tutorials at once. I can make a workspace, tag it, add a deck to it or take one out, and tell you which workspaces a deck is in. A deck stays where it is, so it can be in several workspaces.

> Make a workspace AppSec June 2026, tagged conferences, and add my panel deck to it.

## Review how it went

Oversay keeps each run of a deck: how long every card took against its plan, and the note you wrote on it. With a transcript (from your call app, or your own voice recorded and transcribed on your computer), I can see what was said on each card too. Ask me and I'll write a review: what ran over, what was rushed or skipped, where it wandered, and what to change in the deck. Oversay shows it on the session's page. I don't change the deck unless you ask.

> Review my last stand-up in Oversay.

## Open it, or put it on screen

I can open a deck in Oversay, or show it on screen ready for your call. If Oversay isn't running, it starts.

> Open my catch-up with Sam deck in Oversay and show it on screen.

## Where it works

- **Claude Code, Codex, Gemini CLI and Cowork** write decks straight into Oversay. You don't need Oversay open.
- **Chat in a browser or a chat app** can't reach your computer. There, I give you the deck as a file. Drop it on Oversay's window, or copy it and use **New deck from clipboard** in Oversay's command bar.

## What I won't do

I never delete, rename or move your decks, and I never overwrite one. When I add to a deck, I change only what you asked for.
